# Visual checklist (score each 1–5)

Used by LOOP.md step 6 on every changed screenshot or contact sheet. 5 = would pass for a polished 1990s
commercial RTS; 3 = clearly readable but obviously unfinished; 1 = broken.

1. **Readability at 1× zoom** — can you tell unit types, owners, and resources apart at a glance?
2. **Scale** — units vs buildings vs trees in plausible proportion (a villager ≈ ⅓ of a house's height).
3. **Light & shadow** — one consistent sun direction (front-left); shadows on the ground, same angle everywhere.
4. **Anchoring** — feet/footprints sit on the ground; nothing floats or sinks; sprites align with the tile grid.
5. **Depth order** — nearer objects cover farther ones; no popping as units walk around buildings/trees.
6. **Team color legibility** — every owned object shows its player's color clearly.
7. **Edges** — no halos, jaggies, or stray pixels; outlines crisp at Retina.
8. **Terrain** — no visible tiling grid or repetition; transitions look natural.
9. **Fog of war** — correct states (black / grey / clear), soft edges.
10. **HUD** — legible text, aligned panels, consistent style, nothing overlapping the play area it shouldn't.

Must-fix: any score ≤ 2, or anything that misleads the player (wrong owner color, invisible unit, misplaced pick).
